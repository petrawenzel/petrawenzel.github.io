Petra Wenzel is a Montreal-based artist interested in memory, identity, archive, decay, and their intersections. She works primarily with photo and textile, with a side of printmaking.

When she is not working on current projects, she can be found reading or enjoying the outdoors.

She is the Visual Arts Editor of Ahoy Literary.
